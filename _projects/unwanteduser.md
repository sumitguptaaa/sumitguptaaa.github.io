## Challenge

UnwantedUser is a small network-forensics CTF exercise. The public repository contains a packet capture and the accompanying challenge document.

The intended work is evidence-driven: inspect the traffic, reconstruct what happened, and answer the challenge from the capture rather than from a simulated interface.
